**Status:** Abstract & introduction complete, 5 sections need content

External factors explain 12-15% of performance variance:
1. **Lane advantage:** Lane 8 vs Lane 1 = -0.21±0.08s (outer lanes reduce curve stress)
2. **Environmental effects:** Wind r=+0.066 (weak), altitude r=+0.027 (NS), temperature ~0.08s per 5°C >22°C

## Paper 4: **LIMIT DERIVATION** (The WHY - Theoretical Boundaries)
**Status:** Abstract & introduction complete, 5 sections need content

### Core Argument
Theoretical minimum 400m time = 42.87±0.14s based on:
1. **Hill-Keller energetics:** ATP-PCr depletion + glycolytic flux limits
2. **Mureika curve dynamics:** Two 180° curves impose Δt=+0.20s penalty (Lane 8)
3. **Oscillatory coupling:** Neural-mechanical efficiency degradation under acidosis (κ: 0.88→0.70)
4. **Extreme value statistics:** Sub-43.0s probability <0.001 per athlete-generation

### Key Evidence
- Integrated model: R²=0.82, RMSE=0.16s (comparable to ML biometric models)
- Van Niekerk 43.03s = 98.9th percentile within biological parameter space (0.16s above theoretical minimum)
- Parameter sensitivity: v_max and κ (coupling efficiency) are primary rate-limiters
- Bayesian update (2016-2025 plateau): <4.5% probability sub-43.0s within 20 years
